Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2025
Last Updated: January 2026

AidHandy Inc. (“Aidhandy,” “we,” “us”) operates the website and services available at aidhandy.com. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights. Aidhandy is built on a foundation of traveler safety and "Privacy by Design," ensuring that your journey is verified without compromising your personal booking security.

1. Information We Collect

  • Provided by you: Name, email, phone number, flight details (Airline, Flight Number, Date), and travel preferences.
  • Verification Data: To facilitate seat-proximity matching, we may collect booking metadata or a Passenger Name Record (PNR) code.
  • Support & Profile: Messages sent to our support team and optional profile information (e.g., assistance needs).
  • Payments: Limited billing info handled by payment processors (e.g., Stripe). We do not store full card numbers.
  • Automatically: Device/browser info, IP address, timestamps, and usage analytics for performance and security.
  • From partners: Limited data from seat-intelligence and flight-status APIs to confirm aircraft layouts and cabin configurations.

2. Data Safety & "Verify-and-Purge" Protocol

Because Aidhandy is not a travel agency, we do not need persistent access to your airline reservation. We follow strict protocols to protect your booking:

  • Automated Purging: Once your seat proximity is verified via our seat-intelligence partners, sensitive booking codes (PNRs) are automatically purged or hashed from our active database.
  • Read-Only Access: Our systems are designed to "read" flight data for verification only. We have no "write access" to airline systems and cannot modify, change, or cancel your original booking.
  • Peer Privacy: Your matched companion sees your verified status and seat proximity, but they are never granted access to your PNR, email, or full booking metadata.

3. How We Use Information

  • Seat Proximity Matching: We identify aircraft layouts to verify that travelers and companions are seated within a safe and accessible radius.
  • Booking Verification: Details are used as a one-time "digital handshake" to confirm a live booking.
  • Service Delivery: Facilitating communications between matched parties and providing pre-flight support.
  • Security: Preventing fraud, securing accounts, and complying with legal obligations.

4. SMS and Email Communications

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive service-related SMS regarding bookings and account activity. Message frequency varies based on activity. Pre-flight reminders are typically sent 6 hours before departure. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance.

5. Sharing of Information

We disclose data only to trusted processors under contract:

Category Purpose
Seat Intelligence To provide accurate cabin layouts and proximity reasoning.
Payments (Stripe) To process secure payments and payouts.
Email/SMS Transactional communications (Postmark, Twilio).
Hosting (Supabase) Secure application hosting and encrypted storage.

6. Data Retention & Security

We use AES-256 bank-level encryption for data at rest and TLS/SSL for data in transit. Account records are retained as necessary for operations and legal obligations. Verified flight coordinates are kept only for the duration of the travel event.

7. Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data or its deletion by emailing privacy@aidhandy.com. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under 18. If we learn a minor has provided data without parental oversight, we will delete it immediately.
